Senior Club OS - Help & Documentation

Everything you need to know to manage orders, track expenses, and export receipts.

📦 Managing Orders

Creating an Order: Go to the New Order tab. You can add multiple items to a single order. For each item, select the product type, cotton quality, design, and any addons (like sunglasses or custom packaging).

Cost Calculation: The moment you create an order, the system calculates its total cost based on the current global Settings. Note: If you change global settings later, past orders will NOT recalculate automatically. This prevents old data from being ruined.

Editing an Order: You can edit an order by opening it and clicking the "Edit" button. Only then will the system recalculate its costs using the latest settings.

💰 Payments & The Money Hub

Client Payments: When a client pays you, open their specific order and click + Register Payment. The system will track how much they paid vs how much is still owed.

Order Expenses: Below the payments on the order page, you can add expenses (like a delivery fee). If you select "Out of Pocket", it will actively reduce your net profit for that order.

Money Hub (Payables): This tab gives you a massive birds-eye view of your factory and cotton costs. Instead of tracking what the client owes you, this tracks what you owe to the factory or supplier. You can check off payouts here.

📄 Exporting PDF Receipts

Every order acts as a printable receipt natively out of the box. Simply open an order and click the Download / Print Icon at the top right.

  • The receipt strips away the navigation and buttons.
  • The design elegantly transforms into a customized dark-mode document with a crisp `#2c0000` Senior Club logo.
  • It accurately lists the item breakdowns (including addons).
  • You can choose "Save as PDF" directly from your browser's print dialog to send to clients via WhatsApp or email.

⚙️ Global Settings

The Settings tab controls the base pricing for cotton, factory costs, printing, and addons. Update these immediately whenever your supplier changes their prices. Remember: Changing these does not alter historical orders.

🛡️ System Logs

The Logs tab is your security audit trail. It tracks every payment registered, every order created, and every setting changed—including who did it and when.
